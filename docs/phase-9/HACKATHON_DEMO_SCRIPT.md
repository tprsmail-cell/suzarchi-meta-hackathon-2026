## Detailed Narration Script

### Scene 1: The Circularity Dilemma (0:00 – 0:30)
> *(Visual: Open hero banner showing mountain landscape and Forest Green dashboard)*
>
> "Every single year, over ninety-two million tonnes of textile waste are incinerated or dumped in landfills. The biggest barrier to circular recovery isn't logistics—it's **material opacity**. Without reliable data on fiber composition, yarn count, and hazardous finishes, recyclers simply cannot risk putting secondary materials into high-speed mills.
>
> Welcome to **SUZARCHI** (சுழற்சி)—a Global Circular Material Intelligence Platform built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026. Our mission: *One Material. Multiple Possibilities.*"

---

### Scene 2: Meta Multimodal AI Intake & Honest Evidence (0:30 – 1:05)
> *(Visual: Click 'AI Intake Review' -> Photo of navy fabric roll is evaluated)*
>
> "Intake starts with a single smartphone photograph. Leveraging the architecture of Meta's Llama 3.2 Vision, SUZARCHI analyzes macro weave geometry, surface texture, and packaging format.
>
> But here is what makes SUZARCHI unique: **We never hallucinate certitude.** 
> While the model correctly confirms visible navy color and roll format, it transparently tags fiber composition as `REPORTED` and flags an estimated 180 GSM. It automatically schedules required ISO 1833 chemical dissolution tests before any spinning mill can process it. No silent promotion. Zero guesswork."

---

### Scene 3: Explainable 4-Dimensional Matching & Safety Gating (1:05 – 1:40)
> *(Visual: Navigating to AiMatchAnalysis -> Opening Destination Modal -> Showing Oily Rags mat-007)*
>
> "Once intake is registered, our deterministic circular engine calculates four independent dimensions: Technical compatibility, Regulatory Safety, Missing Evidence, and Commercial viability.
>
> Notice this clean single jersey fabric scores 95% for OE-Rotor Spinning in Tiruppur. 
> But look what happens when hazardous workshop rags (`mat-007`) enter the system: our statutory safety hard gate instantly kicks in, unconditionally blocking commercial textile reuse and unverified solvent laundering to 0%, while safely guiding the material to licensed TSDF cement co-processing."

---

### Scene 4: Digital Product Passport (DPP) & Laboratory Verification (1:40 – 2:15)
> *(Visual: Opening Material Passport -> Donut chart -> ISO 17025 Lab Desk -> Promote Property)*
>
> "Under the European Union's 2024 ESPR directive, secondary commodities require a Digital Product Passport. SUZARCHI features interactive composition breakdowns, recycled content metrics, and a six-pillar regulatory checklist.
>
> When an accredited testing laboratory—such as SITRA with NABL accreditation TC-5482—issues an ISO 1833 blend report, the verification desk matches the cryptographic document hash, promotes the property to `CONFIRMED`, and logs an immutable audit trail."

---

### Scene 5: Multi-Facility Inventory & Batch QR Code Traceability (2:15 – 2:45)
> *(Visual: Switching to Traceability & Batches -> Opening Batch QR Modal -> Printable Mill Label)*
>
> "For enterprise recycling networks, SUZARCHI tracks batches across multiple facilities—from Tiruppur and Panipat to Koper and Lyon—segregating hazardous and quarantined lots from active trading stock.
>
> With one click, mill operators issue an ISO/IEC 18004 compliant thermal QR label encoding a decentralized ESPR URN and a tamper-evident cryptographic hash that any downstream buyer can instantly verify."

---

### Scene 6: Global Scalability & Conclusion (2:45 – 3:00)
> *(Visual: Switching language to Tamil / German; showing carbon offset tCO2e 569,548)*
>
> "With support for 10 languages, 14 currencies, 11 country regulatory profiles, and automated Basel Convention transboundary checks, SUZARCHI connects waste generators to high-value remanufacturers worldwide.
>
> SUZARCHI: Transforming waste into traceable circular value. Thank you."
