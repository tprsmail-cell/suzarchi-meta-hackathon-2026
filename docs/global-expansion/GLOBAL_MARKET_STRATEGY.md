## 1. Global Market Archetypes & Focus Regions

SUZARCHI organizes international expansion across five distinct market groups, recognizing that commercial dynamics, legal jurisdictions, and circular manufacturing capabilities vary dramatically across regions.

```
Global Circular Marketplace Architecture
├── North America (Commercial Demand, Brand Deadstock, High-Volume Post-Industrial)
├── Europe (Regulatory Driver, EU Digital Product Passport ESPR, Advanced Recycling)
├── South Asia (Foundational Manufacturing, High-Capacity Spinning, Artisanal Clusters)
├── East Asia (Synthetic Fibre Excellence, Precision Texturizing, Taiwan/China Hubs)
└── Southeast Asia (Global Garment Export Powerhouses, Vietnam Production Belts)
```

## 2. Granular Market Group Specifications

### 2.1 North America

- **Initial Scope:** United States (`US`), Canada (`CA`).
- **Future Expansion:** Mexico (`MX`) via USMCA manufacturing corridors.
- **Commercial Persona:** Major independent consumption and sourcing powerhouse. Brands and retailers hold massive surplus deadstock, cut-and-sew offcuts in the Carolinas and California, and post-industrial carpets/upholstery.
- **Key Capabilities Required:**
  - Independent US customary units (lb, oz, oz/yd², inches, yards) alongside metric equivalents.
  - Multi-currency pricing in `USD` and `CAD`.
  - Subnational state and provincial hierarchy (e.g., California SB 707 Textile Responsible Collection, North Carolina nonwoven corridors).
  - English and Spanish language interface. Spanish is treated as a core domestic language for US Hispanic textile workers and future Latin American expansion.
  - Strict demarcation between domestic US trade and export consignments.

### 2.2 Europe

- **Initial Scope:** Germany (`DE`), France (`FR`), Italy (`IT`), Spain (`ES`), Netherlands (`NL`), Portugal (`PT`), United Kingdom (`GB`).
- **Future Expansion:** Belgium (`BE`), Poland (`PL`), Türkiye (`TR`).
- **Commercial Persona:** The global pioneer in circular regulation, driven by the **EU Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR)** and the mandatory **Digital Product Passport (DPP)**.
- **Visual Reference Alignment:**
  - Incorporates the **Digital Product Passport (DPP)** model exemplified by `Provenant by Protokol` (see reference image): tracking multi-tier batch movements across European manufacturing centers (Slovenia, France, Italy, Portugal, Spain, Netherlands, Greece, Czech Republic) with automated compliance alignment checks (General Info, Composition, Performance & Care, Environmental Footprint, Certifications).
  - Integrates the **Material Flow and Carbon Offset Tracking** model exemplified by `reneonix` (see reference image): documenting collection points, multi-facility sorting, automated QC scoring, and Scope 3 avoidance telemetry.
- **Key Capabilities Required:**
  - Separate legal configurations for European Union member states and the United Kingdom (post-Brexit UK REACH / Waste Framework divergence).
  - Multi-currency: `EUR`, `GBP`, `CHF`.
  - Metric system standard (kg, m, GSM).
  - Multilingual interface supporting German, French, Italian, Spanish, and English.
  - Integration with EU Waste Framework Directive waste shipment regulations (Regulation (EC) No 1013/2006).

### 2.3 South Asia

- **Initial Scope:** India (`IN`).
- **Future Expansion:** Bangladesh (`BD`), Sri Lanka (`LK`), Pakistan (`PK`), Nepal (`NP`).
- **Commercial Persona:** The world's primary spinning and post-industrial recycling backbone. Home to global super-clusters (Tiruppur, Coimbatore, Panipat, Dhaka, Surat).
- **Key Capabilities Required:**
  - National currencies (`INR`, `BDT`, `PKR`, `LKR`).
  - Languages: English, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Urdu.
  - Direct integration of verified cluster data (e.g., Panipat rag-tearing, Coimbatore rotor open-end spinning, Tiruppur knitwear).
  - Domestic transport networks (rail freight, national highway logistics).

### 2.4 East Asia

- **Initial Scope:** Mainland China (`CN`), Taiwan (`TW`).
- **Future Expansion:** Japan (`JP`), South Korea (`KR`).
- **Commercial Persona:** Global leaders in advanced synthetic textile engineering, polyester depolymerization, functional athletic fabrics, and recycled nylon synthesis.
- **Key Capabilities Required:**
  - **Strict Geopolitical & Linguistic Independence:** Mainland China (`CN`) and Taiwan (`TW`) are configured as distinct, independent markets with neutral ISO identifiers.
  - **Linguistic Separation:** **Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`)** for Mainland China; **Traditional Chinese (`zh-TW`)** for Taiwan. These are linguistically distinct and must never be treated as interchangeable.
  - Local Currencies: `CNY` (Chinese Yuan) and `TWD` (New Taiwan Dollar).
  - Integration of synthetic depolymerization corridors (e.g., Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Changhua, Tainan).

### 2.5 Southeast Asia

- **Initial Scope:** Vietnam (`VN`), Thailand (`TH`), Indonesia (`ID`), Malaysia (`MY`), Singapore (`SG`).
- **Future Expansion:** Cambodia (`KH`), Philippines (`PH`).
- **Commercial Persona:** Vietnam is prioritized as a premier global cut-and-sew apparel export hub (producing millions of tons of pre-consumer knit and woven offcuts for global sportswear brands).
- **Key Capabilities Required:**
  - Vietnamese language (`vi`) as a Tier 1 priority language.
  - Local Currencies: `VND`, `THB`, `IDR`, `MYR`, `SGD`.
  - Port logistics and ASEAN trade corridor tracking (Ho Chi Minh City, Hai Phong, Bangkok, Jakarta).
  - Cross-border export compliance for secondary raw material transfer into India and China.
