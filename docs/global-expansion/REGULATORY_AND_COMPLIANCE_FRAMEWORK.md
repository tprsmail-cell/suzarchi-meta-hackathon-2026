## 1. Modular Multi-Jurisdiction Compliance Model

International circular material commerce is governed by overlapping, often conflicting national and international environmental laws. SUZARCHI establishes a **modular regulatory compliance engine** that checks material characteristics against relevant regional legal frameworks without issuing unauthorized legal advice:

```
Regulatory Jurisdiction Modules
├── International: Basel Convention on Transboundary Movements of Hazardous Wastes
├── European Union: Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR) & Waste Framework
├── United States: Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA) & FTC Green Guides
├── India: Hazardous & Other Wastes Rules 2016, Bio-Medical Waste Rules 2016 (CPCB/TNPCB)
└── ASEAN: Regional environmental trade protocols & transboundary waste controls
```

## 2. Immutable Universal Safety Directives

Under no circumstances can an international user, commercial buyer, or regional policy override SUZARCHI’s universal safety constraints:

1. **Healthcare Infectious Textiles (`CAT-MED-BED-INF`):**
   - Universal Prohibition: Direct commercial recycling, sorting, or apparel resale of potentially infectious clinical linens is strictly prohibited globally.
   - Statutory Route: Segregation for licensed Common Bio-medical Waste Treatment Facilities (CBWTF) or medical waste autoclaving/incineration.
2. **Petroleum & Solvent Contaminated Rags (`CAT-WRK-OIL-HAZ`):**
   - Universal Prohibition: Mechanical garnetting, shoddy spinning, artisanal weaving, or municipal landfilling is strictly prohibited due to spontaneous combustion and machinery fouling.
   - Statutory Route: Industrial cement kiln co-processing (Alternative Fuel and Raw Material - AFRM) or licensed hazardous waste TSDF destruction.
3. **Broken Needles & Metal Fragments:**
   - Precondition: Mechanical recycling pathways are locked until 100% electromagnetic needle detector inspection certifies zero ferrous fragments (>0.8 mm).
4. **ETP Dyehouse Chemical Sludge (`CAT-WET-SLD-HAZ`):**
   - Universal Prohibition: Agricultural land application or textile pulping is strictly barred. Permitted solely for hazardous secured landfill stabilization (SLF).

## 3. Privacy, Data Governance & Cross-Border Data Flows

- **Data Minimization:** Only physical textile attributes, certified test reports, and registered commercial entities are indexed. Personal user data is segregated.
- **Cross-Border Transfer Restrictions:** Complies with EU GDPR (Chapter V international data transfers) and Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023.
- **Trade Secret Protection:** Proprietary blend formulations, exclusive buyer contracts, and mill pricing are encrypted in-transit and restricted to bilateral negotiation channels.
